WHO WE ARE

Our story and calling.

Christ Gospel Mission International was founded on 10 August 1969 in Lagos, Nigeria, under the leadership of Rev. Daniel Olagoke Ogunkanmi. The church began with a commitment to proclaim the whole counsel of God and the unadulterated Gospel of Jesus Christ.

The mission continues to help people know God's Word for themselves, deepen their relationship with Him and use their gifts to spread the Gospel. CGM Karu belongs to this wider family of churches.

OUR MISSION

Proclaim the Word.
Raise leaders.
Reach the nations.

Christ Gospel Mission International is committed to proclaiming the unadulterated Word of God with signs and wonders, planting vibrant churches in all nations of the world, and developing leadership until Jesus Christ comes back.

A story of faith.
Still being written.

From a beginning in Lagos to a growing family of churches, these moments trace the wider Christ Gospel Mission journey.

1969

Christ Gospel Mission begins

On 10 August, Rev. Daniel Olagoke Ogunkanmi led the founding of Christ Gospel Mission in Lagos, Nigeria.

1982

Faith on campus

Rev. Victor Ogunkanmi and James Oluloro began CGM Students' Fellowship at the University of Ife, engaging young people in faith and fellowship.

1988

The mission reaches Abuja

On 3 July, Rev. Victor and Rev. Olubukunola Ogunkanmi pioneered the first Abuja CGM congregation, beginning from their living room.

2013

A new chapter

Rev. Victor Ogunkanmi succeeded the founding General Overseer, with his commissioning at CGM's 44th Annual Convention.

TODAY

Growing together in Karu

CGM Karu shares this continuing mission through biblical teaching, worship, prayer, discipleship and the fellowship of believers.
