WHO WE ARE 01

One family.
One purpose.

Our story is part of something bigger. Founded in Lagos in 1969, Christ Gospel Mission International continues to proclaim Christ and nurture communities of faith. Here in Karu, Abuja, that mission lives through worship, prayer, discipleship and care for one another.

01 / MISSION

Our mission

To faithfully proclaim God's Word, establish thriving churches across nations, and raise leaders who serve Christ as we await His return.

THE GOSPEL AT THE CENTRE
02 / PHILOSOPHY

Our philosophy

We believe there is room for everyone to grow, belong and serve. Faith is lived out in community, through learning, relationships and meaningful service.

OUR ROOTS 02

A story of faith.
Still being written.

From a beginning in Lagos to a growing family of churches, these moments trace the wider Christ Gospel Mission journey.

1969
THE BEGINNING

Christ Gospel Mission begins

On 10 August, Rev. Daniel Olagoke Ogunkanmi led the founding of Christ Gospel Mission in Lagos, Nigeria.

1982
A NEW GENERATION

Faith on campus

Rev. Victor Ogunkanmi and James Oluloro began CGM Students' Fellowship at the University of Ife, engaging young people in faith and fellowship.

1988
EXPANDING THE MISSION

The mission reaches Abuja

On 3 July, Rev. Victor and Rev. Olubukunola Ogunkanmi pioneered the first Abuja CGM congregation, beginning from their living room.

2013
LEADERSHIP & CONTINUITY

A new chapter

Rev. Victor Ogunkanmi succeeded the founding General Overseer, with his commissioning at CGM's 44th Annual Convention.

TODAY
THE STORY CONTINUES

Growing together in Karu

CGM Karu shares this continuing mission through biblical teaching, worship, prayer, discipleship and the fellowship of believers.
