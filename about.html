Our roots

A story of faith, since 1969.

Christ Gospel Mission International is the wider church family to which CGM Karu belongs. Its story began in Lagos, Nigeria, on Sunday, 10 August 1969, under the leadership of Rev. Daniel Olagoke Ogunkanmi. The mission was founded around a commitment to preach the whole counsel of God and the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

1969

Christ Gospel Mission begins

CGM was planted in Lagos on 10 August with Rev. Daniel Olagoke Ogunkanmi leading the new mission.

1982

A growing student fellowship

Rev. Victor Ogunkanmi and James Oluloro began CGM Students' Fellowship at the University of Ife, extending the mission's reach among young people.

1988

The mission reaches Abuja

Rev. Victor and Rev. Olubukunola Ogunkanmi pioneered the first Abuja CGM church from their living room on 3 July 1988. Their ministry helped establish other congregations in the region.

2013

A new chapter of leadership

Rev. Victor Ogunkanmi succeeded the founding General Overseer, with his official commissioning completed during CGM's 44th Annual Convention in August 2013.

Today

One mission, many communities

CGM continues to share the Gospel, develop leaders and build communities of faith. Here at CGM Karu, we carry that mission forward through worship, Bible teaching, prayer and fellowship.

These dates describe the history of CGM International and its Abuja expansion, not a separately confirmed founding date for the Karu congregation. Official CGM history · Leadership
